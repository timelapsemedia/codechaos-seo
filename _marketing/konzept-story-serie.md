# Concept: Animated story series "FROSTFALL" (faceless, voice-over only)

As of 08.11.2026. Based on the format analysis `format-analyse-oskar-schnee.md`. The animated-story principle has been carried over; the story, world, characters and plot are entirely our own.

## Goal (three revenue streams)
1. **Plugin sales on Gumroad** (Code Chaos Audio: Black Metal Suite, drum kit, Chaos FM, Crucible, banks)
2. **Mastering jobs** on polished.media (packages from 79 €)
3. **Social media income itself:** TikTok Creator Rewards (videos > 1 min, from 10,000 followers), later YouTube

## Communication rules (fixed)
- The videos **never** say that plugins or masters are made with AI. They also don't say how plugins are built. The story and the sound speak for themselves.
- Conversely, we **don't claim** "no AI" or "all handmade". Statements about the manufacturing process are left out entirely.
- **AI-generated images/videos** in the story are labelled via the platform settings: TikTok "AI-generated content", Meta "AI info", YouTube "altered or synthetic content" where it applies. This only concerns the visuals, not the products. Platforms require it and penalise missing labels.
- Every claim about products (number of samples, formats, price) matches the Gumroad page exactly.

## Why this works
- Oskar's data: Instagram and TikTok carried the reach; serial cliffhangers drive follows; the hook needs action immediately; ~45 % retention was enough to go viral. **Sound is our advantage:** his videos have music beds, ours have a sound that is itself part of the plot and can then be bought.
- Black metal / dark fantasy is visually strong, has a loyal niche with high willingness to buy (bands, home studios), and few faceless story accounts serve it.
- The story world produces sounds naturally: cold choirs, tremolo guitars, blast beats, bells, ice. Each episode has a "sound of the episode", which leads to the product.

## Series premise (our own)
**FROSTFALL.** In a remote northern village there is a legend: whoever records the "sound under the ice" gets everything they wish for, and loses their voice. Liv, 19, guitarist in a band nobody listens to, finds an old reel-to-reel tape in her late grandfather's ice cellar. On it is a recording that should not exist. With every part she plays more of the tape, and with every part the village changes.
- **Tone:** cold, quiet, eerie, not splatter. Everything is shown through sound: when the tape plays, the image freezes.
- **Running gags/motifs:** the tape counter (episode number), frost on the speaker, a raven that appears whenever a new sound is heard.
- **Season 1:** 12 parts of 61–75 s (just over the 1-minute mark for Creator Rewards). Season 2 in the city (the band, the label, the "deal"), which naturally sets up mastering.

## Episode structure (every part, 61–75 s)
| Time | Element |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | **Action immediately:** a picture that raises a question (tape starts by itself, ice cracks). No title card. |
| 2–8 s | One-line context: "Part 3. Liv has played the tape for the second time." |
| 8–50 s | Story beats, image change every 2–4 s, voice-over tight in the present tense |
| 50–60 s | **The sound of the episode** plays clearly (3–6 s, dry vs. "frozen"); this is the product moment, without naming a product |
| 60–70 s | Cliffhanger plus "Part 4 tomorrow". On-screen text: "Sound from part 3 → link in bio" |

Caption: first line a question from the story; then 2–3 sentences; then "The sounds from FROSTFALL: link in bio"; 3 hashtags.

## Funnel
1. **Story account** (new, its own brand "FROSTFALL", like Oskar with his story account) → bio link to a hub page.
2. **Hub page** (subpage on codechaos-official.de or polished.media, e.g. `/frostfall`), with three buttons:
   - "The sounds from the series" → Black Metal Suite / drum kit on Gumroad (free demos first as the entry point)

## Episode 1 (rough script)
- **0–2 s:** Close-up: an old tape machine in an ice cellar. The reels begin to turn. Nobody is in the room.
- **Voice-over:** "Nobody in Frostvik touches Grandpa's tape. Liv did it anyway."
- **Beats:** Liv's band plays in front of three people. The grandfather's funeral. The cellar key in his coat pocket. Frost on the stair. The machine under a blanket. A label in his handwriting: "Do not play. Never."
- **50–60 s:** She presses play. Silence. Then a choir that sounds as if it were frozen. Image freezes, the raven lands on the window sill.
- **Cliffhanger:** "When she looked up, her breath was no longer visible. Part 2 tomorrow."
- **On-screen:** "Sound from part 1 → link in bio"

## Production (tools available)
- **Story/scripts:** written by Tim himself (his voice, his world). Claude helps only with structure and hook variants.
- **Images/clips:** skill `ki-medien` (cost cap per episode). One consistent visual style (cold blue/black, film grain, copper-coloured accents as the Code Chaos signature), plus character sheets for Liv so she stays recognisable.
- **Voice:** one fixed narrator voice (a TTS service via `ki-medien`, or Tim's own voice if he wants). Calm, low, German.
- **Sound:** **real** sounds from the plugins and packs. The sound of the episode is rendered from the products, so the promise in the bio is kept.
- **Editing/motion:** skills `code-chaos-motion` / `motion-reels` (captions, transitions); final encode −14 LUFS.
- **Posting:** existing pipeline (Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Buffer → TikTok/X/Threads). Note: TikTok Creator Rewards needs the account directly on TikTok; check whether posting via Buffer counts.

## 7-day launch sprint (like Oskar, measured)
| Day | What | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Set up account + hub page, produce parts 1–3 in advance | — |
| 1 | Part 1 + 3 trial reels with different hooks (Instagram) | 3-s hold, retention, follows/1,000 views |
| 2–5 | One part per day, at the same time | Which hook wins → standard for the next parts |
| 6 | Halftime: sound breakdown (product moment) | Clicks on hub page, demo downloads |
| 7 | Review: views, followers, clicks, demo downloads, sales, mastering requests | Decision: continue / adjust |

Only these KPIs count: **follows per 1,000 views, hub clicks, demo downloads, sales, mastering requests.** Views alone are not a goal.

## Risks and how we address them
- **Story fatigue** (Oskar: views dropped from day 9) → seasons of 12 parts, visual upgrade after the first slump, comments as a source of ideas.
- **Image logic errors** (Oskar had them pointed out in comments) → check every image before publishing (skill `stock-sichtung` / review sheet).
- **Missing AI labels** → platform setting on every post (checklist).
- **Brand separation:** FROSTFALL is its own account; Code Chaos / Polished appear only in the bio and hub, so the brands remain clean.
