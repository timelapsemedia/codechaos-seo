# FROSTFALL: Season 1 (Polished Metal Mastering)

**Channels:** existing Polished accounts (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts). Bio link → hub page `polished.media/frostfall`.
**Format:** animated story, faceless, German narrator voice, 61–75 s per part, 9:16. One part per day at the same time (suggestion 19:00).
**Goal:** Polished mastering jobs (free mix check as the entry point), Black Metal Suite on Gumroad (Code Chaos Audio), TikTok Creator Rewards.

## Style rules (borrowed from viral story formats)
1. **Second 0 is action.** No title, no logo, no "hello". The first image raises a question.
2. **One sentence per image.** Image change every 2–3 s, voice-over in short present-tense main clauses (max. 12 words).
3. **Part counter top left** ("Part 3") as the series' recognition mark. No "Part 1" in the first image of part 1 (it shouldn't look like the start of a long series).
4. **A curiosity gap every 10 s:** something is hinted at but not explained.
5. **End mid-movement** (cliffhanger), never with a summary. Last image: the raven or the tape counter.
6. **Sound of the episode** at second ~50: 3–6 s clean, the image freezes (frost effect), on-screen text: "This sound → link in bio". No product name in the video.
7. **Release logic:** the first 3 parts arrive within 24 h (cliffhanger binge), then one part per day.
8. **Captions** in large words, 2–3 words per insert, keywords in ice blue.
9. **Never** a statement about how plugins or masters are made. No AI statement about products.
10. **AI label** on every post via the platform setting (images only), plus a review sheet against image errors (hands, text, continuity).

## Visual style (for all image prompts)
Cold Nordic dark fantasy, painted look between graphic novel and film still, colours ice blue / black / bone white, single copper accent (Polished/Code Chaos signature: tape reels, eyes of the raven). Film grain, slight vignette. **Liv:** 19, black, half-shaved hair, rust-red wool scarf (recognition mark), old Telecaster-style guitar with a crack in the body. **Grandfather Eirik:** white beard, sailor's cap. **The raven:** one eye copper. Build character sheets for Liv, Eirik and the raven before part 1 (skill `ki-medien`).
