# LAST RAVE: Season 1 (Code Chaos)

**Channels:** existing Code Chaos accounts (Instagram @codechaos_official, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X/Threads via Buffer). Bio link → hub page `codechaos-official.de/last-rave`.
**Format:** animated story, faceless, German narrator voice, 61–75 s, 9:16, one part per day (suggestion 20:00; on release day parts 1–3 within 24 h).
**Goal:** Chaos FM, Crucible, Kick+Bass Engine, Shardstep, Hitech Arsenal and preset banks on Gumroad; TikTok Creator Rewards.

Same style rules as FROSTFALL (`serie-frostfall.md`): action at second 0, one sentence per image, part counter, curiosity gap every 10 s, cliffhanger, sound of the episode at ~50 s, no statement about how plugins are made, AI label for the images.

## Visual style
Neon noir in the rain, painted, like a graphic novel: night city in black/petrol, light sources **copper-orange** (Code Chaos signature), pink only for warning lights. Strobe/glitch effects in time with the BPM. **Kai:** 22, undercut, worn copper-coloured headphones around the neck (recognition mark), old laptop with stickers. **Mira:** DJ, silver bob, mirror sunglasses even at night. **The silence patrol:** grey uniforms, a decibel meter instead of a badge.

## Premise
**Neo-Hamburg, 2049. The city council has limited music to 120 BPM.** Anything faster counts as a "disturbance of public peace". Kai produces at night under his blanket, through headphones, at 200 BPM. One night a pirate frequency plays a track that shouldn't exist: 220 BPM, and everyone who hears it dances until sunrise. Kai wants to know who's behind it, and gets drawn into the last illegal rave of the city.
