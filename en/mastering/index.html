Why genre-specific mastering?

At 180 BPM there are 333 ms between two kicks; at 240 BPM only 250 ms. A limiter that works fine on house music will flatten the transient, smear the bassline into the kick and push dense hitech leads into harshness. Mastering psycore, hitech and darkpsy means making a track loud while keeping three things intact:

  • The kick: short, punchy and clearly separated from the bass, even at high tempo.
  • The bassline: rolling neurotrance or darkpsy basses that stay defined on club systems and earbuds.
  • The atmosphere: pads, drones and FX with depth instead of a flat wall of sound.

Code Chaos has produced these genres since 2016 and masters other artists' releases with the same precision as the own catalogue. No presets, no one-click AI mastering: every track is checked against references from its genre.

How it works

  1. Send a request with genre, number of tracks, deadline and a reference track.
  2. Get a reply with details and the upload link, usually within 24–48 hours.
  3. Upload your files: WAV or AIFF, 24 or 32-bit, no limiter on the master bus, peaks around -6 to -3 dBFS. Stems aligned to the same start point.
  4. Receive your master as a 24-bit WAV plus MP3 preview, ready for Bandcamp, SoundCloud, Spotify and labels.
  5. Revisions are included: one for stereo, two for stem mastering.

Mastering FAQ

How much does psytrance mastering cost?

Stereo mastering is €79 per track including one revision. Stem mastering is €129 per track for up to 8 stems including two revisions. Packages with cover art and video start at €149; a full album with 10 tracks, cover art and a promo video is €999.

What is the difference between stereo and stem mastering?

With stereo mastering you send one finished mixdown. With stem mastering you send up to 8 groups (for example kick, sub, bass, leads, pads, FX), so problems in single frequency ranges can be fixed directly. For dense psycore and hitech mixes, stem mastering usually gives the cleaner, louder result.

How should I prepare my files?

Export WAV or AIFF at the project's sample rate, 24 or 32-bit, without a limiter or clipper on the master bus and with peaks around -6 to -3 dBFS. Stems should start at the same position and be exported with all their effects. Add a reference track if you have a sound in mind.

Do you master genres other than psytrance?

Yes. The focus is psycore, hitech and darkpsy, but electronic music in general (techno, drum and bass, industrial, experimental) is welcome. Just mention the genre and a reference in your request.

How do I pay and receive my master?

After your request you get a reply with the details, usually within 24–48 hours. You receive a 24-bit WAV master plus an MP3 preview. Revisions are included as listed for each service.
