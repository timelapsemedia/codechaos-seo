What is Darkpsy?

Darkpsy (also dark psy, dark psytrance or dark psychedelic trance) is the dark side of psytrance: 150 to 200 BPM, usually around 155–175 BPM. The genre works with heavy, rolling basslines, disturbing samples, an occult atmosphere and themes of night, death and transcendence. Darkpsy is the foundation from which hitech and psycore evolved.

By Code Chaos (Tim Borchert), producer from Hamburg · updated 5 Oct 2026

Origins

Darkpsy developed in the early 2000s as a dark counter-movement to melodic full-on. Germany and Russia played a big role; the German artist Xenomorph is regarded as one of the early pioneers of the dark, occult aesthetic in psytrance. Strong scenes in Brazil and Latin America followed.

The faster, more experimental styles grew out of darkpsy: hitech raised tempo and complexity, psycore pushed tempo and hypnosis to the extreme. If you want to understand those genres, start with darkpsy.

What does darkpsy sound like?

  • Bass: heavy and rolling; the frequency balance is often shifted downwards: lots of sub, fewer highs.
  • Kick: punchier and more accented than in psycore.
  • Samples: horror film quotes, whispers, screams, sounds of nature and the night.
  • Atmosphere: long reverb-drenched pads, menacing drones, dissonant chords.
  • Themes: darkness, death, madness, the occult and transcendence.
"Darkpsy was the first thing that showed me music doesn't have to be pleasant to be unforgettable."Code Chaos

Darkpsy, forest and gothic

Darkpsy overlaps strongly with forest psytrance, which sounds more organic and nature-bound. With the single "Uhrwerk aus Blut" (Clockwork of Blood, 30 Oct 2026), Code Chaos combines darkpsy and hitech energy with gothic atmosphere and spoken German scenes: a heart that beats too fast, a clockwork that bleeds.

Darkpsy DJs and artists to know

A few names that shaped the darkpsy sound and are still played on dark floors today (a selection, not a complete list):

  • Xenomorph (Mark Petrick, Berlin), German pioneer of the dark, occult and often gothic aesthetic
  • Kindzadza (Moscow), one of the defining names of the Russian dark sound
  • Fungus Funk (Moscow), releasing dark and forest psytrance since 2001
  • Psykovsky, a key figure of the Russian dark and forest scene
  • Para Halu (Hungary), dark and forest psytrance with a very distinctive, playful sound design
  • Baphomet Engine (Brazil), darkpsy from the strong Latin American scene
  • Cosmo, German producer between darkpsy and early hitech
  • Code Chaos from Hamburg, darkpsy with gothic atmosphere ("New Psychedelic Death Art")

Many of these artists also play DJ sets. For faster names from the same circle, see the hitech guide and the psycore guide.

Darkpsy festivals: where can you hear darkpsy live?

Darkpsy is mostly played at forest and dark open airs, underground parties and on the night floors of big psytrance festivals, often in the hours after midnight. In Germany, for example, the Mushroom Stage of Indian Spirit Festival is known for harder, experimental sounds. Earlier there were live sets, for example at Abstract Ritual Hamburg (2023) and Lost Signal Festival (2026); today Code Chaos is a studio-only project.

Producing darkpsy: notes from the studio

  1. The bassline carries the track. Work on envelopes and filter movement until the bass sounds menacing on its own.
  2. Use samples sparingly but deliberately. One strong sample in the right spot does more than ten in the background.
  3. Depth over loudness. Use space, delay and foreground/background so the atmosphere can breathe.
  4. Warmth in the low end. Gentle tube or tape saturation thickens the bass without distorting it, separately per band, for example with Crucible.
  5. Master for dynamics. Darkpsy lives on contrast. Darkpsy mastering should reach loudness without flattening the atmosphere.

FAQ: Darkpsy

What BPM is darkpsy?

Darkpsy runs between 150 and 200 BPM. Typical tracks sit around 155–175 BPM; faster releases at 190–200 BPM approach hitech.

What is the difference between darkpsy and psytrance?

Psytrance is the umbrella term (mostly 138–150 BPM). Darkpsy is a faster, darker subgenre with heavy basslines, horror samples and an occult atmosphere.

Is darkpsy the same as forest?

No, but they are closely related. Forest sounds more organic and nature-bound; darkpsy is more menacing, more technical and more horror-driven.
