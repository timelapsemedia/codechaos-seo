Gravesaw: black metal amp sim with built-in cab

Gravesaw is a black metal amp sim: an amp with a built-in cabinet. Plug in a guitar or bass DI and get the cold, sawing, saturated tone of the second-wave records: tremolo picking that stays clear even at 170+ BPM, highs that cut instead of hiss, and no clean headroom at all. Gravesaw is the amp of the Code Chaos Black Metal Suite.

Black metal amp sim features

  • Noise gate before the amp (threshold from −90 dBFS = off to −30 dBFS, with hold and hysteresis), so the gaps between tremolo bursts stay silent
  • Tight: high-pass before the distortion, 20 to 400 Hz, for a tight low end or a full doom foundation
  • Gain stages with 4x oversampling and anti-aliased clipping. Raw / Thick moves the voicing from raw and sawing (harder clipping, more highs into the distortion) to thick (softer clipping, push in the low mids)
  • Tone stack after the distortion: Bass, Mid, Treble (±12 dB each), Fizz (the range above about 4 kHz, from tamed to full fizz) and Presence
  • 19 built-in cabs: 10 guitar cabs (4x12, 2x12 open back, 1x12 lo-fi combo, a wide L/R pair for doubled rhythm guitars), 6 tamed versions and 3 bass cabs. Switching crossfades without clicks. With cab off you load your own IR after Gravesaw
  • Low Blend mixes the clean low end of the DI under the distortion, for bass
  • 16 factory presets: Cold Tremolo Lead, Carpathian Buzz, Wall Rhythm L / R (+ Tamed), Tamed Fizz, Four-Track Demo, Frozen Wall Thick, Depressive Haze, Low Gain Crunch, Bass Grind, Bass Clank, Doom Bass Deep and Amp Only (own IR)
  • Input and output meters in dBFS, gate indicator. Mono or stereo input

The cabs are synthesised, not miked. They are the impulse responses from the Black Metal Tone Kit, designed in code from a model of speaker, cone breakup, mic position and enclosure. They are not copies of commercial cabinets or IR libraries. Every cab is level-matched, so switching changes the tone, not the volume.

Pairs with the Black Metal Drum Kit and the atmosphere bus Rimehall (page in German).

Tests, formats and systems

What was tested

Every preset is rendered with a synthetic DI riff in the automated tests: no clipping (all peaks around −4.5 dBFS; a safety stage at the output keeps peaks below −0.3 dBFS even with a DI 12 dB hotter), no DC, no silence. Latency is 61 samples (1.4 ms at 44.1 kHz) and is reported to the DAW; the cab convolution adds none. In the worst tested case (2.8 kHz sine at full gain, rawest setting, cab off) aliasing sits 54 dB below the harmonics.

Formats and systems

Windows 10/11: VST3 + standalone. macOS Apple Silicon only (arm64, no Intel build): VST3 + standalone, unsigned and not notarized. macOS blocks the plugin on first launch; allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security. No AU, so it does not run in Logic Pro. Direct support from the developer: if anything acts up in your DAW, message me and I will help. No license server, no login, no online check.

Gravesaw FAQ

Does Gravesaw work for bass?

Yes. Low Blend mixes the clean low end of the DI under the distortion, there are 3 bass cabs and the presets Bass Grind, Bass Clank and Doom Bass Deep.

Can I use my own impulse responses?

Yes. With cab off (preset Amp Only) only the amp runs, and you load your own IR after Gravesaw.

Are the cabs copies of famous cabinets?

No. The cabs are synthesised: impulse responses from the Black Metal Tone Kit, designed in code from a model of speaker, cone, mic position and enclosure. They are not copies of commercial cabinets or IR libraries.

Does Gravesaw run on a Mac?

Yes, but only on Macs with Apple Silicon (arm64); there is no Intel build. The plugin is unsigned and not notarized: macOS blocks it on first launch, allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security. There is no AU format, so it does not run in Logic Pro.

Do I need an account or online activation?

No. There is no license server, no login and no online check.

Is there a free demo?

Yes. The Gravesaw demo on Gumroad is the full plugin with a short noise burst every 45 seconds.
