3-band saturation features

  • 3-band processing: independent low, mid and high control with two adjustable crossovers. Drive your bass hard while keeping highs clean
  • Five saturation types, chosen per band: Hard Clip (aggressive, loud, surgical), Wavefolder (metallic, synthetic, alien), Tube (warm, rounded, musical), Diode (asymmetric, gritty bite), Tape (glue, smear, vintage drive)
  • Per band Drive, Mix, Gain, Mute and Solo, plus output gain and bypass
  • Oversampling up to 8x for clean, alias-free saturation even when you push hard
  • Designed by an active hitech and psycore producer for real sessions: mix, master, destroy

How it works

  1. Split: set the low/mid and mid/high crossover points to carve your signal into three independent bands.
  2. Saturate: choose a saturation type per band and push drive. Add weight to lows, bite to mids, air and edge to highs.
  3. Balance: blend with per-band mix, trim gain, solo to audition, and use oversampling to keep it all clean.

Why 3-band saturation?

Harmonic saturation adds musical overtones to a sound, so it feels louder, warmer and more present. Instead of saturating the whole signal at once, Crucible gives every band its own character: a hard clip into sub and kick for low-end punch, a gentle tube on the leads in the mids, tape on the highs, all in a single instance. Crucible was forged for the fast and the dark, but works anywhere you would reach for a saturator: drum & bass, techno, dubstep, hip-hop, rock and mastering.

Good to know

Crucible re-checks the license in the background every two weeks and keeps working offline for up to 60 days. Until activated it behaves like the free demo.

Windows 10 or newer: VST3 + standalone, in any VST3 host such as REAPER, Ableton Live 12+, Cubase 12+, Studio One 6+, FL Studio and Bitwig. There is no macOS build of Crucible. See the Gumroad page for the latest system requirements.

Crucible FAQ

What is Crucible?

Crucible is a 3-band harmonic saturation plugin by Code Chaos Audio. It splits your signal into low, mid and high bands and lets you saturate each band independently with five selectable types (Hard Clip, Wavefolder, Tube, Diode, Tape), per-band Drive, Mix, Gain, Mute and Solo, and oversampling up to 8x.

Is it good for psytrance, hitech and psycore?

Yes, it was built by an active hitech, psycore and darkpsy producer. Add warmth and weight to kicks and basslines in the low band while adding bite and presence to leads and percussion up top, without muddying the mix.

What's the difference between the saturation types?

Hard Clip is aggressive, loud and surgical, great for pushing low-end punch. Wavefolder folds the waveform back on itself for metallic, synthetic overtones. Tube is warm, rounded and musical for mids. Diode adds asymmetric, gritty bite. Tape adds glue, vintage smear and air. You can assign a different type to each band.

Does Crucible run on a Mac?

No. Crucible is a VST3 plugin and standalone app for Windows 10 or newer; there is no macOS build.

How does license activation work?

Your Gumroad receipt contains a personal license key. Paste it into Crucible once (Activate button, internet needed); one key activates up to 3 computers. Crucible re-checks the license every two weeks and keeps working offline for up to 60 days.

Is there a free demo?

Yes. The Crucible demo on Gumroad is the full plugin with a short noise burst every 45 seconds.
