How it works

  1. Apply on Gumroad: one short form where you say where and how you plan to recommend.
  2. Once approved, you get your personal link for every paid product.
  3. Share it in videos, courses, posts or your Discord. Every purchase within 30 days of the click counts.

Who it is for

Anyone who helps others produce: YouTube and Twitch channels, teachers and courses, Discord and forum communities, labels and blogs around psytrance, hitech, psycore, darkpsy and sound design. A recommendation works best when you show the plugin in a real track.

Terms

  • 30 % commission on all paid products (plugins, preset banks, sample packs, templates). Free demos are excluded.
  • 30-day cookie from the click on your link.
  • Billing, tax and weekly payouts are handled by Gumroad. You need a Gumroad account.
  • Every application is approved individually. No minimum term, stop any time.
